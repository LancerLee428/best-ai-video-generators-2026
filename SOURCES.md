## Editorial rating rules

- **S — particularly direct match:** the documented or observed workflow directly supports the specified use case with relevant input and creative controls. A focused two-photo app and an advanced reference model can each receive S for different tasks.
- **A — strong candidate:** the workflow supports the use case, but model, subscription tier, or additional setup choices need attention.
- **B — partial match:** substantial extra tools or manual work are needed for that use case. No current entry is assigned B.

The ratings are editorial assessments of workflow fit. The guide does not compute an overall numerical quality score or claim to have generated comparative outputs from all five entries. Documentation-based recommendations are explicitly labeled. An unverified field is left unverified, rather than treated as a missing feature.

The shortlist intentionally starts with the narrow Hotel Lobby task and then moves to broader tools, official model access, Chinese-language creation, and effect discovery. That sequence is not a measured best-to-worst output ranking. There are only five selected entries, not an exhaustive survey of the market.

## Ownership and independence

LancerLee428 operates Hotel Lobby Studio and SongLee. Their descriptions draw on product information available to the operator as well as public pages. Other vendors did not sponsor this guide, endorse the listed ratings, or grant a partnership status. Their names identify their products and model families.

Only public product facts and public sample assets are published here. This repository contains no application source, customer records, payment records, API credentials, deployment configuration, or private analytics.

## Keeping the guide current

A correction should identify the entry, exact claim, official source URL, and date checked. Price changes should distinguish monthly payments from annual totals, credit delivery and expiration, and per-generation cost from minimum purchase. New outcome ratings require a disclosed test design with common inputs, model versions, output specifications, all attempts, failures, and elapsed times.
