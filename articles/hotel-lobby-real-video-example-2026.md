## Four useful things to compare

First, look at each person's face throughout the clip, rather than judging likeness from the poster alone. Then compare outfits and placement. Watch how the generator builds the booth and performance around the references. Finally, listen to the audio and decide how you would prepare the clip for publication.

This makes the example valuable for a purchase decision. You are inspecting a complete take, including its details and imperfections, with the source photos available beside it.

The audio is generated by AI and varies between results. It is not the original Hotel Lobby song. Add music you are permitted to use in an editor or posting platform if your project calls for a particular track.

## Prepare your own first take

Use clear reference photos of two consenting adults, with visible faces and sufficient detail. The studio accepts JPG, PNG and WebP up to 10 MB per photo, with a minimum of 256×256 pixels. Performer 1 is directed to the left and Performer 2 to the right.

Choose the sample's 10-second Standard configuration if you want the same requested duration and quality. The [$5 pack](https://aihotellobby.online/pricing) contains the required 120 credits. Your result can still differ in likeness, motion, framing and audio; one sample does not establish a universal success rate.
