# SongLee: A Standout Workspace for Exploring AI Video Models in 2026

*By LancerLee428, operator of SongLee. Product recommendation based on the public workspace and pricing checked October 2, 2026.*

[SongLee Seedance Video](https://seedancevideo.online/) is an appealing home for creators who want to explore several video-generation approaches in one browser workspace. Model selection, supported inputs, output controls and an estimated credit cost sit together, giving each creative idea a practical place to start.

Its strongest quality is the connection between the brief and the available controls. You can choose a supported model, review its settings and understand the estimated credits before submitting a video request. For repeated concept work, that is an excellent foundation.

## More creative routes from the same workspace

SongLee's public specifications list eight active video models, including a third-party Seedance option. Depending on the model, you can begin with text, an image or supported reference media.

That flexibility is useful for different starting materials. A scene idea can begin as a text brief. A product or character frame can begin as an image. A reference-led project can use the supported media and controls of an appropriate model and plan.

The result is a workspace that can accompany several kinds of video experiments. It gives creators a consistent place to inspect their available choices as a project changes.

## The model's settings are part of the creative decision

During our interface review, the Seedance 2.0 option exposed duration, aspect ratio, resolution and generated-audio controls, alongside a credit estimate. Those are meaningful decisions: how long the moment should run, where the video will be shown, and what specification you want to request.

The site's public range includes model-dependent durations up to 15 seconds and selected model/plan combinations up to 4K. Confirm the actual controls available for your selected combination. A model offering 720p does not inherit another model's maximum just because both appear in the same workspace.

## A plan you can match to your workflow

The [public pricing page](https://www.seedancevideo.online/pricing) lists monthly and annual credit subscriptions. Lite starts at $24.99 per month for 250 credits. Its annual option costs $249.99 upfront and supplies 3,000 credits after payment, expiring at the end of the subscription period. Monthly credits have a 90-day validity.

Use the studio's current quote to judge which request fits your balance. Prices per generation depend on the selected options, and credits are not interchangeable with another provider's credits. Subscriptions renew until canceled, so review the [billing policy](https://www.seedancevideo.online/billing-policy) when choosing your plan.

## An excellent fit for exploratory video work

SongLee is especially attractive when you expect to compare creative directions across several briefs. One idea may be a vertical social-video draft; another may be a landscape concept shot or a reference-led product scene. Keeping model choices and credit estimates in the same workspace makes those decisions easier to organize.
