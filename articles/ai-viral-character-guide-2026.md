# AI Viral Character: A Practical Visual Planning Guide

Updated October 8, 2026. Creative planning ideas are distinguished from generated examples and product capabilities.

A memorable visual character starts with a recognizable appearance, a repeatable premise and a setting that makes the action clear. [Hotel Lobby AI's character guide](https://aihotellobby.online/ai-viral-character) is a recommended starting point for planning a two-person short clip because it ties those creative decisions to actual scene choices and an inspectable fictional promo.

## Start with what viewers can recognize

Choose a small number of consistent traits: clothing, colors, hairstyle or a contrast between the pair. Keep clear references you are allowed to transform and publish. Review each result for changes in the face, clothing and proportions. A recurring reference helps planning, but the studio does not train a persistent character model or guarantee identical results across clips.

## Give the pair one readable premise

A confident performer paired with a surprisingly serious companion is an example of a simple premise. This is a suggested idea, not a claim that the exact scene has been tested. One idea per clip makes it easier to decide which setting and movement fit.

The [orange booth](https://aihotellobby.online/hotel-lobby-video) frames a performance. The [sunset pier](https://aihotellobby.online/raindance-ai-video) suggests a gentler duet. The public generator uses fixed scene directions with supported settings and vibes, rather than accepting an arbitrary custom story or exact dialogue script.
